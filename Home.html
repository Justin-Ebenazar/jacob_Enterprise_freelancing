How It Works

Our system uses your device's camera to analyze both sign language gestures and behavioral patterns. The process is simple, private, and designed to be accessible to everyone.

  1. Allow camera access when prompted
  2. Perform signs or natural movements
  3. Receive instant translation or analysis
  4. View personalized recommendations

The system works in real-time with no need for expensive hardware - just your smartphone, tablet, or computer.

Educational Tips & Resources

Sign Language Basics

Learn the fundamental signs that can help you communicate basic needs and feelings. Start with simple signs like "eat", "drink", and "more".

Sensory Activities

Engage in sensory play activities that can help with focus and emotional regulation. Try textured materials or calming sensory bottles.

Communication Strategies

Discover alternative communication methods like picture exchange systems or speech-generating devices.

Routine Building

Create visual schedules to help establish predictable routines that can reduce anxiety and improve independence.

Social Interaction

Practice social stories and role-playing to help understand social cues and appropriate responses in different situations.

Learning Techniques

Implement multi-sensory learning approaches that engage different senses to enhance understanding and retention.
